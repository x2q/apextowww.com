The Apex Domain Problem

An apex domain (also called a naked domain) refers to your domain without the "www" prefix. Most modern cloud hosting platforms prefer or require websites to be served from a www subdomain because of several technical constraints:

  • DNS Limitations: Apex domains can only use A/AAAA records pointing to specific IP addresses, while www subdomains can use CNAME records that point to another hostname, offering more flexibility.
  • High Availability: Using www allows services to change underlying server infrastructure without requiring you to update DNS records.
  • CDN Integration: Content Delivery Networks work more efficiently with www subdomains, improving your website's global performance and reliability.

Popular Services With www Requirements:

  • GitHub Pages
  • Netlify
  • Vercel
  • Heroku
  • Firebase Hosting
  • Cloudflare Pages

Frequently Asked Questions

What is apex domain redirection?

Apex domain redirection automatically forwards visitors from your naked domain (example.com) to your www subdomain (www.example.com).

Is SSL supported?

Yes, we automatically provision SSL certificates through Let's Encrypt to ensure secure HTTPS redirections.

How much does it cost?

Our service is completely free with no hidden costs or subscription fees.

Why should I use www instead of an apex domain?

Using www provides better CDN compatibility, more flexible DNS options, and improved reliability with services like GitHub Pages, Netlify, and Vercel.

How long does redirection setup take?

Setup is instant, but DNS propagation can take 5 minutes to 48 hours depending on your previous DNS configuration and provider.

Will this affect my SEO?

No, proper 301 redirects are search engine friendly and will transfer your SEO value from the apex domain to your www subdomain.

Does this work with all TLDs?

Yes, our service works with all top-level domains including .com, .org, .net, country-specific TLDs, and newer extensions.

Are there any bandwidth limitations?

There are no specific bandwidth limitations for regular usage, but for extremely high-traffic domains, please contact us.

Can I redirect to a non-www subdomain?

No, this service is specifically designed to redirect from apex domains to www subdomains only.
