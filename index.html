What is an apex domain?

An apex domain, also called a root, naked or bare domain, is a domain name with no prefix in front of it: example.com, not www.example.com. DNS does not allow a CNAME record at the apex, so most hosts cannot serve it directly. A redirect to www solves that.

Set it up in three steps

Your www record stays at your host. ApexToWWW only takes over the apex.

  1. 1

    Add the A record

    At your DNS provider, create an A record for @ (the apex).

    A  @  65.21.184.101
  2. 2

    Add the AAAA record

    Add an AAAA record for @ so IPv6 visitors get the same redirect.

    AAAA  @  2a01:4f9:c012:a304::1
  3. 3

    Test the 301

    After DNS propagates (typically 5 to 30 minutes), check the response.

    curl -I http://yourdomain.com

Remove any existing A or AAAA records on @ first. Keep your www CNAME pointing at your host. Not sure how your host handles www? Pick your provider below →

In one sentence: ApexToWWW is a free DNS-based service that 301-redirects an apex (naked) domain to its www subdomain. You add one A record (65.21.184.101) and one AAAA record (2a01:4f9:c012:a304::1) at the apex, and every request is answered with a 301 to the matching https://www URL, over an automatically renewed Let's Encrypt certificate.

Why a redirect, and not ALIAS or CNAME flattening?

ALIAS, ANAME and CNAME flattening serve your site on the apex directly, but only some DNS providers offer them. A redirect works with every provider and keeps your real traffic on www.

Frequently asked questions

What is an apex domain, and why does it need a redirect?

An apex domain (also called a root, naked, or bare domain) is your domain without any prefix: example.com rather than www.example.com. DNS does not allow a CNAME record at the apex (RFC 1034), so it cannot point at most hosts and CDNs, such as GitHub Pages, Netlify and Vercel. A redirect sends apex visitors to your www subdomain, where your site lives.

How do I redirect my apex domain to www with ApexToWWW?

Add an A record for @ pointing to 65.21.184.101 and an AAAA record for @ pointing to 2a01:4f9:c012:a304::1. There is nothing to sign up for or configure. DNS changes typically propagate within 5 to 30 minutes.

Do I need my own SSL certificate?

No. ApexToWWW issues a Let's Encrypt certificate for your apex domain on the first HTTPS request and renews it automatically. Your www site keeps the certificate from your own host.

Is ApexToWWW really free?

Yes. There is no account, no usage limit and no paid tier today. It runs on small ARM64 servers, which keeps costs low.

Does the redirect keep the URL path and query string?

Yes. example.com/pricing?plan=pro redirects to www.example.com/pricing?plan=pro. It is a 301 (permanent) redirect, so search engines treat the www URL as the canonical address.

Which hosting providers does it work with?

Any host where www is a CNAME, including GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, Heroku, Firebase Hosting, Render and AWS Amplify. Keep your www record pointing at your host. ApexToWWW only handles the apex.

Does ApexToWWW redirect www to the apex instead?

No. It only redirects the apex to www. To go the other way, use your host's own redirect feature. Our guide to redirecting www to apex covers Cloudflare, Vercel, Netlify and other hosts.

Will the redirect affect my SEO?

A 301 tells search engines that www is the canonical address, which consolidates ranking signals on one URL. Keep a canonical tag on each page pointing at the same www URL, and list only www URLs in your sitemap.

Are wildcard domains supported?

No. The free service only redirects domains whose A and AAAA records point directly at its servers. That is verified before a certificate is issued. Wildcard domains are not supported.
